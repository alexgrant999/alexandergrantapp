# PRODUCT.md

## What this is

alexandergrant.app is the public face of Alexander Grant's consulting practice, plus the client back office behind it (invoicing, portal, subscriptions). The homepage's single job is to convert prospective clients: independent online retailers and multi-location local businesses who need someone to grow their revenue and build the systems behind it.

## Register

brand

## Users

Owners of small-to-mid retail and service businesses, not developers. Real examples: the owner of an Australian outdoor apparel brand on Shopify (met through referral, evaluates on revenue numbers), the founders of a NYC children's activity studio group (evaluate on reliability and how the product feels to their families). They open the site from a proposal email or a WhatsApp message, usually on a phone, and decide within a minute whether Alex looks like someone worth a monthly retainer.

## What Alex sells

An ongoing growth partnership: paid advertising (Google, Meta), SEO, email and SMS automation, and custom-built platforms (bookings, ecommerce, portals) when off-the-shelf tools hold the business back. Proof is year-on-year revenue numbers reported to clients monthly.

## Brand voice

Plain-spoken, seasoned, commercial. Three physical-object words: a shopkeeper's ledger, a well-kept country store, a firm handshake. Numbers are the star; adjectives are suspect. First person singular ("I run your ads"), never agency-speak ("we leverage synergies").

## Tone rules

- Lead with client outcomes, never with the tech stack.
- Short sentences. No em dashes or en dashes anywhere, ever.
- Technical terms only where a shop owner would use them (Shopify, Google Ads, email).

## Anti-references

- Developer portfolio aesthetics: dark hacker themes, monospace labels, version-number tags, status pips. The audience does not read READMEs.
- Generic agency landing pages: stock handshake photos, "we scale brands", three-icon feature rows.
- Corporate consultant navy-and-white templates.

## Strategic principles

- Real numbers from real engagements beat any amount of copy. Client-approved figures only.
- One call to action: start a conversation (WhatsApp or email). No pricing pages, no funnels.
- The site itself is a work sample. It should feel considered and fast.
